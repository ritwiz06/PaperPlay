## The rule

The model has no chance in it, so a level's stated effect is checked on 20 starts: the world's own values, and 19
others with each parameter within a tenth of its value. It must hold in its stated direction on at least 16 of the 20,
and show within 20 s of watching at 4x. Both worlds of a fork run from the same start, and the What-if sets its
variables to their stated values in each. Both are sampled every 0.6 days. A level that fails is reworked or cut, never
softened in wording. A count claim, which states the surprise itself, must be exactly right.
