# Flock defaults sweep

Generated by `npm run sweep:defaults` (scripts/sweep-defaults.ts) on node 24.21.0 with
flock.planar.weighted@2.1.0. Deterministic: rerunning it on the same commit rewrites this file
and the CSV files byte for byte. ADR-0003 records the decision this informs.

## Method

- Fixed at the prototype's values: 72 birds on a 32 × 32 torus, separationRadius 1.55,
  targetSpeed 2.7, speeds 0.45–3.6, maxAcceleration 4.6; no beacons or obstacles.
- Each configuration ran 20 seeds (1–20) for 1200 steps (20 s at 1/60 s).
- A seed succeeds when, at the last step, directional agreement ≥ 0.8 and the flock is one
  group (clusterCount = 1). "Median s to order" is the first once-per-second check at which
  that held.
- A configuration qualifies with at least 11 of 20 successes. The choice is the
  qualifying configuration with the most successes, then the one closest to the prototype
  (1.2, 1, 0.8, 4.5) in grid-normalised distance, then the earliest in grid order.

### Stage "initial" (256 configurations)

A bracketing grid around the prototype's settings, with alignment capped at 1.5 so a default leaves room to raise it.

separationWeight ∈ {0.6, 0.9, 1.2, 1.5}, alignmentWeight ∈ {0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5},
cohesionWeight ∈ {0.4, 0.8, 1.2, 1.6}, perceptionRadius ∈ {4.5, 6, 7.5, 9}.
Best in this stage: 10/20 successes. Rows are in flock-defaults-initial.csv.

### Stage "extension" (192 configurations)

Declared after the initial stage, whose best configurations sat on its boundary (alignment at its top, cohesion at its bottom, perception at its top) and of which none qualified. It extends one step past each of those boundaries and refines separation around the best value, 0.9.

separationWeight ∈ {0.75, 0.9, 1.05}, alignmentWeight ∈ {1.25, 1.5, 1.75, 2},
cohesionWeight ∈ {0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4}, perceptionRadius ∈ {7.5, 9, 10.5, 12}.
Best in this stage: 14/20 successes. Rows are in flock-defaults-extension.csv.
