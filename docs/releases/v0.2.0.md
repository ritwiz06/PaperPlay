# v0.2.0: G2, a verified authored world

G2's gate, "Verified authored world", passed on the commit this tag names: `npm run check:gate`
with G2 current, every check run, the flocking world reviewed against its source map, and R01 to
R08 and R12 each with passing evidence. The gate's output is in this tag's message and in
`docs/sessions/2026-10-18.md`.

## What G2 delivers

- **The flocking world, reviewed:** `fixtures/worldspecs/flocking-reference.json`, six levels from
  Reynolds's "Flocks, Herds, and Schools" (1987), every anchor a short quote located in the paper by
  its source map and checked against a fresh fetch; four statements reworded to say only what the
  paper says (ADR-0045).
- **The game-first shell** (ADR-0008): the stage filling the screen, the level card with its one
  control and its What-if, the guide, the level map, the depth toggle, the paper journal with
  two-way provenance, origin chips named in words.
- **Learning tools:** a prediction before every What-if, never scored (ADR-0023); a split-screen fork
  with one measured difference charted (ADR-0019); guided exploration; a speed control that keeps a
  run bit for bit (ADR-0020).
- **The level rule:** every level's stated effect checked on 20 skies before anyone sees it
  (ADR-0018).
- **The deterministic builder,** interpreting a WorldSpec against the primitive registry (ADR-0016).
- **Accessibility:** an axe scan, WCAG 2.0 to 2.2 at A and AA, on every world's states at a desktop
  size and at 360 px (R12).

## Deferred, and why

- **`/world/local` in the shell, and the replay export and import UI, with `npm run replay` and its
  tolerance report.** The owner moved them after G3's first generated world on 2026-10-09: replay
  across worlds is worth building once more than one kind of world exists. R07 is partly evidenced:
  records replay bit for bit in the tests, but a learner cannot yet export or import one.
- **The stale-evidence rule,** that a new primitive version or source hash resets the checks that
  depend on it. Moved into G3 on 2026-10-09, since generated worlds are what depend on versions.
- **Reading notes and learning questions for the flocking paper.** Not written: the source map
  carries the claims the world needs, and the questions belong with G4's evaluation protocol.
- **The flocking paper's section files,** four for development and eight held out. Moved from G2 on
  2026-10-09; G4 now evaluates on papers from three domains (ADR-0021), so whether they are needed is
  decided when G4's protocol is frozen.
- **The deploy:** connecting the static deploy is the owner's step (ADR-0017); the security headers
  are written and tested against the built app.
- **A cross-runtime capture check,** Mac and Linux frames within a stated tolerance: later, since
  the canonical capture is Linux's (ADR-0010).
- **Threshold Town's source map:** the Royal Society's site refuses this machine; the owner supplies
  Kermack and McKendrick's PDF, and its map comes first when it arrives (ADR-0045). Threshold Town
  stays a draft, labelled as one.
