## Decisions made and needed

**Made by the owner:** every decision in the plan for every paper (ADR-0024).

**Made by me:**
- **G3a's branches are named `g3/<topic>`,** because land accepts only `g<1-5>/<topic>`.
- **The town kit, the shell for any world, and Threshold Town (ADR-0029):**
  - forks start from the start in every family but agents, so a compartment fork always shows
    the difference the level rule checked;
  - a run with an end gets Start again; the words of restarts and of the rule's 20 runs follow
    the family;
  - the sweep's neighbourhood: factors from `mulberry32(k)`, the What-if's values exact;
  - four days of the model a second at 1x, so a 150-day level takes 9.4 s to watch at 4x;
  - Threshold Town's five levels and their claims, written to the solver's numbers; every value
    a teaching choice; no teaching value inside a paper-derived statement;
  - the fixture repeats its model in each level, and the app refuses a world whose levels bind
    different models.
- **The compartment family (ADR-0028):** a model as data, one solver registered as a solver, its
  four checks run by the builder on both worlds of every fork, the tolerance capped. A
  subagent wrote the core to my specification; I reviewed it before taking it.
- **worldspec/3 (ADR-0027):** its fields; `design.world` kept as the scene kit's id; version 2
  still read and upgraded on read; the current version's schemas carry a `V3` suffix, because
  the version 2 names are what the unchanged tests build.
- **Scene kits (ADR-0026):** three halves in their own layers; a generic frame of the stage's
  part and a kit's typed layers, not the plan's closed list of layer kinds; the base adapter
  keeping the creation order; the kits' Three.js halves in the stage's one chunk; a generic
  session with a kit's view; the fork spawning its worlds.
- **Primitive families (ADR-0025):** the families' catalogue as data in contracts; the five new
  descriptions on the primitive interface; the flock as the agents family's first primitive,
  with its own entry point. Deferred, with reasons: open entity kinds and positions beyond
