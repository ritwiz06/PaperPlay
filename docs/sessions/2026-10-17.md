## Decisions made and needed

- **Two planners judged before any words:** the rules need only the plan and the model, so both
  plans are built and swept with placeholder words, and the explainer writes for the kept plan
  alone (ADR-0043).
- **The second reviewer is left unchosen on this branch:** the report proposes gemma4-31b-it, but
  the reviewers' bake-off runs again on the review rules' new prompt; the pick follows it.
- **The direct-effect tag reads the derivative's sign at the paper's values,** for each product
  of compartments: δ(1 − θ)c expands to δc − δθc, yet adds to cQ while θ is below one (ADR-0044).
- **A lone reviewer's block stands where no second reviewer is chosen,** so the rule degrades to
  one reviewer, not none (ADR-0044).
- **The conflict on hepatitis C's level 5** (emergent, yet ruled a fault) is scored as the owner
  ruled in the bake-off and handled as rule 3 says in jobs; the regenerated world ships its mirror
  image. The owner decides which gives way (ADR-0044).
- **Reynolds's paper from his own site** (the owner): registered as ACM's copyright and the
  author's copy, its text not kept; the copy is his 1995 OCR reprint, without figures, which may
  differ from the print by a character (ADR-0045).
- **Kermack and McKendrick 1927 not fetched:** the Royal Society says every article over 70 years
  old is free to read, but its site answers this machine with 403, page and PDF alike; getting past
  that is not ours to do. Threshold Town waits for the owner's browser download (ADR-0045).
- **Four flocking statements reworded** to what the paper says, rather than anchored to passages
  that do not say them (ADR-0045).
- **A share world's claim from its data, not the planner's sentence:** rewriting numbers inside a
  model's sentence is fragile; the level's data already holds the relation, the amount and the
  summary's label (ADR-0042).

  tokens thinking and wrote nothing, and the retry, sent the empty reply to correct, broke the
  guide's `{to}` rule; now such a reply is asked again with twice the room, for the explainer only,

  goal stopped mid-word, "The vaccinated group's o", at the schema's 200 characters under
  constrained decoding; the rewrite is now bounded in words and must end a sentence.
- **The /create captures lost their storyboard** during the repairs; the jobs ran on in the API and
  were followed through their event files instead.

- **A share at a sentence's end was missed:** "above 0.01." ended in a full stop, which the pattern
  read as part of the number; it now looks for a digit after the point.
