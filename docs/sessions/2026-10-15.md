## Problems and how they were solved

- **The Mac slept through a land, then ran on battery in Low Power Mode.** `g3/transcribe`'s
  third land failed when macOS put the machine to sleep for 15 minutes ("Dark Wake Thermal
  Emergency", lid closed) in the middle of the browser tests. Today the battery was full but
  Low Power Mode was on; I waited for the load to settle and ran each land under
  `caffeinate -i -s`, which keeps the Mac from sleeping only while the land runs.

- **The bake-off was unfair to slow models until four transport and parsing faults were found,**
  each fixed for every model alike and the bake-off resumed from its recordings: Node's fetch
  gave up after 300 seconds; the gateway's proxy ended silent replies with 524; schema-held
  replies looped on whitespace or thought until their limit; and a gateway's decoding turned
  LaTeX's `\frac` into a form feed and `rac`. Every failed call was retried once with the fix.
- **The first transcriber's time unit was wrong:** mimo-v2-6-flash wrote "day" for all three
  papers, and the match ignored time units. They count now, and mimo fell to eighth.
- **The first jobs stopped, each saying why:** I cancelled the first tuberculosis job on seeing
  its model per day; the second stopped as `needs_information` on quotes the gateway's decoding
  had corrupted. On the hepatitis C paper, the planner's repair ran out of room at the
  controller's old 12,000-token limit, and then the reviewer thought past 8,000 tokens and wrote
  nothing; both failed the job, as a failed stage should. The limits are fixed; the time unit,
  the restored commands and the second transcription's check have tests of their own.
- **The Mac slept with its lid closed at 16:23** and the planners' calls in flight dropped
  (`ENOTFOUND`, `ETIMEDOUT`); `caffeinate` cannot hold a closed lid on battery. Resumed.
