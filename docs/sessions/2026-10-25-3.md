## Problems and how they were solved

- One head reached 100%: the task depends only on the sum, which an even average keeps. Narrower
  models (4 and 8 wide) did not learn it; an asymmetric task's probe separates one head from two.
- The first run's manifests said "+changes", read after the run wrote its own results: the stamp now
  reads the run's inputs alone, and the run was repeated.
- Float32 and float64 differ by up to 1.3e-5 of a row's largest score, scores reaching 155: the
  score tolerance is relative to the row.
