## Problems and how they were solved

- **The trails could not be seen.** The cause was the flock's slow cruise, not the drawing
  (see Checks).
- **The lake hid under a hillside.** The hills are now carved into a flat basin around it.
- **The meadow's edge was a sawtooth,** because it was coloured per face. It is now blended
  per vertex, and the hills keep their faceted shading.

- **`649a59e` mixed the workflow change with its docs,** against `CLAUDE.md`'s "commit docs
  separately". It was already merged and pushed, so it was left as it is; later commits are
  split.
