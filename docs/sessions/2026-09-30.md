## Acceptance conditions, in plain language

1. **RunRecord v2.**
   - Every event is exactly one of param, place or remove, with no extra keys.
   - A record whose observable series do not hold floor(totalSteps / sampleInterval) + 1
     samples is rejected.
   - A run that places and then removes a beacon replays bit for bit from its JSON.
2. **Defaults.** The sweep is deterministic. It chooses defaults under which most of its
   seeds end 20 s as one ordered group, and it records the result honestly, including
   what it does not show.
3. **Stage.**
   - Drawing the flock under any camera or palette, or not drawing it at all, leaves every
     observable bit-identical.
   - The Three.js adapter can neither read nor write simulation state.
   - The page draws a non-blank frame under software WebGL, and explains itself when WebGL
     is missing.

## What failed along the way

- **RunRecord checks piled on noise.** Zod runs refinements even after range failures, so
  for `sampleInterval: 0` my series check divided by zero and added two misleading issues.
  Derived checks now wait for valid inputs.
- **Two test expectations were wrong:**
  - An unknown event kind is reported at `events.0.kind`, not `events.0`.
  - The adapter tests asked for 9-digit precision from 32-bit instance buffers; they now
    use 5 digits, while the camera matrices (64-bit) keep 9.
- **The sweep's initial stage chose nothing:** its best was 10 of 20, at the grid's
  boundary. The extension stage was declared after that (see ADR-0003).
- **One full sweep run crashed at its last step, losing 8.5 minutes of computing.**
  - Cause: the held-out check received a whole sweep row, result fields included, and the
    strict parameter schema refused them.
  - Fix: only the four rule settings are copied, a new test reproduced the crash first, and
    each stage's table is now written as soon as that stage completes.
- **The `stage-core` tests were written first but not run red** before the
  implementation. To compensate, 12 bugs were planted and each was caught.
- **Lint caught three things:**
  - unsafe `any` from Three.js `instanceof` narrowing and from `expect.closeTo`;
  - unused omit-a-key names in tests;
  - a deliberate unbound method in the fallback test, now annotated with its reason.
- **three 0.186 removed `PCFSoftShadowMap`;** using it only logs a warning, so the stage
  uses `PCFShadowMap` with a radius.
- **The first screenshots** showed a camera too close, hills too steep and near, and a
  phone view that cropped the field. The fixes were a wider default view, lower hills with
  fog, larger birds, and a tested portrait fit.

## Decisions and deviations

- **Primitive versions.**
  - 2.0.0 changes the state contract, because entities now carry ids.
  - 2.1.0 changes only the defaults.
  - 1.0.0 was never tagged or persisted, so it was retired rather than kept for replay.
- **RunRecord v2 is slightly stricter than asked:**
  - no events after the last step;
  - no double place, double remove or remove before place;
  - at least one observable series.
- **The stage reads two states,** plus the interpolation fraction and the timestep, rather
  than one. The bank angle needs the turn rate, which needs two consecutive states.
- **The sweep needed three readings and two additions:**
  - "within 20 s" means still ordered at 20 s;
  - "most" means at least 11 of 20;
  - ties go toward the prototype's settings;
  - a declared second stage;
  - a held-out check.
- **Birds are drawn in ink, not the prototype's coral,** which is not a token.
- **WebGL is forced onto SwiftShader** in Playwright, and the test asserts that it was.
