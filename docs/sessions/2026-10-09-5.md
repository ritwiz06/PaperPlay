## Goal

The owner's answers, a new rule, then four slices, each on a short branch off `main`.

**The rule (ADR-0018).** A level's stated effect must hold in its stated direction on at least
16 of 20 seeds, and show within about 20 s, or within the level's time at 4x. Measure before
writing it into a fixture, record the table in `eval/sweeps/levels.md`, and make a test re-run
it. A level that fails is reworked or cut, never softened in wording.

  a draft.

  tested against the built app.
- **The Reynolds PDF:** coming. Slice 1 of the source map runs as soon as it is there.

**Slices:**
1. The level rule, and the rework of levels 2 and 4.

3. Guided exploration, with level 2's prediction first.
4. A speed control (1x, 2x, 4x), with a 4x run replaying bit for bit against 1x.

Stop after slice 4, or earlier if something needs the owner's decision. Show:
- screenshots of the fork and the prediction, on desktop and phone;

Acceptance conditions, in plain language:
- **The rule:** every level's stated effect holds on at least 16 of 20 skies, or level 2's
  count is exactly right. `eval/sweeps/levels.md` records it, and a test re-runs it.
- **The gate check:** G2 fails while `flocking-reference.json` is a draft or holds a
  placeholder.
- **The CSP:** the built app plays with no policy violation and no console error, served with
  `vercel.json`'s headers.
- **The fork:**
  - two worlds on the same seed, side by side on a desktop and stacked on a phone;
  - the level's measured difference as a chart over time;
  - "Both worlds restart" where the builder derives it;
  - axe clean on both sizes.
- **Guided exploration:**
  - level 2 asks its prediction before the fork opens, and reports the 20-sky result
    afterwards;
  - nothing is scored.
- **Speed:** 4x takes four times the fixed steps per frame, and a run at 4x replays bit for bit
  against the same run at 1x.

## Problems and how they were solved

- **Level 5 failed the rule.** It was reworked by measurement, not softened.
- **Re-serialising the oracle fixtures reformatted 20 hand-written files.** They were
  restored, and the edits redone as text replacements, so the diff is only what changed.
- **The sweep was 2.4 times slower under Vitest's module transform.** The test runs it in
  Node, and level 6's time came down from 80 s to 40 s, where its claim still held on 20 of
  20 skies.
- **A strict CSP reported Zod's eval probe.** Zod's `jitless` option is now set first; the
  policy was not loosened.
- **A browser check expected an empty attribute** where the stage had not set it yet. It
  now checks for no ring.
