## Problems and how they were solved

- **The PDF the owner meant to add is missing.** Slice 2 and the docs do not need it, so they
  go first.
- **The owner's measure failed the owner's first criterion** (1.28%, upper bound 1.52%). I
  did not loosen or reinterpret the criterion. I measured alternatives (a larger minimum or a
  tighter angle made it worse) and asked; the owner chose O1.
- **The hook-seed drift test failed** once the measure changed: the rule now picks seed 5.
  I asked rather than re-pin; the owner chose to follow the rule.
- **A planted bug escaped** (exactly 45°); a sharper boundary fixture now catches it.
- **Two of the level script's expected effects did not hold** when run (levels 2 and 4). The
  fixture states what the model does, and the question goes to the owner, not into the text.
- **A builder test failed for a good reason twice:**
  - renaming a shared action's variable is caught by the schema first;
  - a whole-number variable refuses a fractional step.

  In both, the test was narrowed, and the builder left alone. A placement outside both worlds
  was reported twice; it is now reported once.
- **A commit was about to break the build:** a staged deletion slipped into the session
  commit. The two commits were regrouped before pushing, and the first was checked on its
  own: typecheck, then 397 tests passed.
- **The React Compiler lint refused** a `useCallback` over derived values, then a ref
  written during render. The refresh became a plain function that reads the level from a
  ref set on entering it.
- **The first commit order left a commit that failed the gate.** I regrouped the slice into
  two commits and checked that the first passes on its own.
