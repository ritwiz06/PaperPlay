regenerations and a plan, on short branches landed through `npm run land`:

   recomputes the headline quantity from the transcribed equations (for R0, the next-generation

   computed observation; a journal page, "Running the paper's own numbers", shows the check's table.
   If even the direction does not show, the first anchored finding the model shows, saying why. A
   mistake of ours found by the check is fixed and recorded as a finding about the pipeline.

   never dropped; any other level that fails its repair is dropped, the reason in the job; in a
   repair the planner sees its control's row of the response table.

## Decisions made and needed

- **The locks live in the git directory,** not in the home directory or the system's temporary one:
  every worktree of the repository shares it, which is where gates and live jobs run, and nothing is
  written outside the repository (ADR-0039).
- **The reproducibility check's findings** (ADR-0040): on the hepatitis C paper, the model's own
  R0 and equation (7) agree, 0.7622553, at Table 1's values, every value is as written, nothing
  else stands behind Figure 7, whose caption says it holds the other values "constant as displayed
  in Table 1", and across adherence 0 to 1 R0 runs between 0.648 and 0.776: the mismatch is
  confirmed, and it is the paper's figure, not our transcription, that the table does not
  reproduce. On the tuberculosis paper, both of its formulas agree with the model's own equations
  (Rv 33.7; R0 80.4 with vaccination off), every value is as written, and its stated "R0 = 2.67
  for the estimated model parameters" follows from neither.
- **Words, not a stop, for a stated value that does not follow:** the tuberculosis paper's 2.67 is
  in a finding, not its headline, so the page says it as a computed observation and the job goes
  on; only a headline about R0 with a likely mistake of ours stops a job.
- **Both papers are worlds, on the reviewer the declared rule picks** (ADR-0035's fourth
  amendment, ADR-0041): hepatitis C and tuberculosis are ready on glm-5-3, every level meeting
  the level rule, the visibility rule and the findings check.
- **Needed from the owner: what counts as definitional.** gemma4-31b-it, the reviewer before,
  blocked as definitional levels that test what the papers report (tuberculosis: faster treatment
  lowers the infectious) and once rightly (hepatitis C's level 5: raising the quitting rate fills
  the quit compartment); glm-5-3 blocks neither, and that level 5 is in the ready hepatitis C
  world. Options: a level that tests a finding the paper reports, in the paper's direction, is
  never definitional; a definitional flag must name the equation term it follows from; or two
  reviewers from different vendors, a block standing only where both agree. And whether a
  reviewer's "unsupported" should send a level back: glm-5-3 found tuberculosis's level 2 worded
  as if contact pulls people out of the vaccinated, which the model does not do; today it is
  advice.
- **The reviewers' world gained two levels with no fault** (ADR-0035's fourth amendment): with one,
  a reviewer that blocks everything scored as well as one that blocks only the faults.
- **A job's token budget, 1,200,000:** five levels mean five explainer calls a round; the
  tuberculosis job stopped at 604,060 of 600,000 before its last round (ADR-0041).
- **The bake-off plans on the picked value set,** as a job does: it had planned the tuberculosis
  paper on its synthetic example.
- **`pipeline:live serve` holds its lock while it runs, idle or not:** a server that can start a
  live job at any moment counts as running one, so land waits for it to be stopped.

## Problems and how they were solved

- **The sweep took 30 seconds a paper:** each of its 101 points found the disease-free rest by
  running the model there. Newton's method first settles a rest linear in the uninfected in one
  step; the sweep now takes 30 milliseconds.
- **A threshold read as a result:** "a backward bifurcation at R0 = 1" was taken for a stated
  value; a stated 0 or 1 is now a threshold, not a result.
- **The rebase met the rules branch's docs** in the architecture's table; I took `main`'s and put
  this branch's rows back.
- **The jobs ran three times on changing picks:** first on qwen36-27b while glm-5-3's last plans
  were out, then on kimi-k2-7-code with gemma4-31b-it reviewing, then with glm-5-3 reviewing once
  `design-v5` picked it. Each rerun replayed every call already made; only new calls went live.
- **The jobs first ran on the old planner pick.** To save time I started them while glm-5-3's
  last plans were still out; `design-v4` then picked kimi-k2-7-code, and I stopped them and ran
  them again. Their calls on qwen36-27b are set aside, not committed.
- **The tuberculosis job ran out of tokens** before its last explainer round; with the budget
  raised, both jobs ran again, replaying every call already made.
- **A long storyboard on a phone at twice its pixels** is taller than the browser can capture; the
  phone storyboards are taken at their own size.
- **Counts in the millions read badly:** "13568699.92 people", and an axis clipped to "00000". The
  readout now writes them whole with separators, and the axis "30M".
- **The dev server held its port** after I stopped it by name; I freed it by its port.
