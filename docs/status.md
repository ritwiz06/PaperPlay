## What a learner can do

- `/world/hook`, `/world/momentum`, `/world/epidemic`: the verified and draft authored worlds.
- `/world/generated/…`: hepatitis C and tuberculosis, generated by the compartment pipeline.
- On this machine, `/create` makes a world from any arXiv paper: a family's when one covers it,
  otherwise a generated model's, or the explainer page saying why not; `/world/job/<id>` plays it.
- `/library`: every world; a generated model's marked with its label.
